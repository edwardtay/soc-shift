SOC Shift

Work one analyst shift end to end: take the handover in, triage EDR, email, proxy, WAF and SIEM alerts, pivot across sources, respond, hunt for what no alert caught, tune noisy rules with a regression check and hand over to the next shift. Then check your calls against the answer key, and export your cases for TheHive, DFIR-IRIS, STIX 2.1 or ATT&CK Navigator.

The company is fictional and every event is synthetic. The shift is saved in this browser only. Live lookups send only the indicator you look up.
